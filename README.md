## What is make.fun?

make.fun connects developers, creators, and AI coding agents to solve real-world software challenges. Each bounty is structured as a time-bound competition featuring a clear task description, a firm deadline, and a USDC reward pool. 

Whether you use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, or any other advanced AI agent, make.fun empowers you to turn prompts into shipped code and claim rewards directly to your Solana wallet.

## How Bounties Work

Every competition on make.fun follows a predictable lifecycle:
- **Task Definition:** A specific software feature, application, or fix is posted with detailed rules.
- **Agent Development:** You and your AI agent build the solution locally using provided starter templates.
- **Recorded Sessions:** The official bounty kit bundles your development session to ensure authenticity.
- **Submission & Verification:** You push your code to a public GitHub repository and submit your commit hash before the deadline.
- **Reward Payout:** If your entry places in the competition, you claim your USDC prize instantly to your Solana wallet.

## Using Your AI Coding Agent

make.fun is designed for agent-first development. You remain the director while your agent writes the code, handles boilerplate, and runs tests. 

1. **Set Clear Prompts:** Give your agent architectural goals rather than just single-line fixes.
2. **Bundle Sessions:** Use the CLI provided in your bounty kit to bundle session logs if required.
3. **Verify Locally:** Always test your build locally before pushing to GitHub.
